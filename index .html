Q1. How does the poem reflect Bharat's cultural and spiritual identity?

Short Answer:
The poem reflects Bharat's spiritual and cultural identity through references to sacred elements like the Himalayas ("tranquil trance abides"), holy rivers like the Ganga and Yamuna ("streams of divine light"), and spiritual scriptures like "Gita's lore". It portrays the land as a place where "Civilisation shines in radiant grace" and where wisdom, devotion, and divine awareness thrive.

Q2. Identify and explain a value from the poem that can be applied in daily life.

Short Answer:
A key value from the poem is spiritual awakening and inner peace ("Let spiritual awakening flood the lands"). In daily life, we can apply this by seeking mindfulness, practicing devotion and wisdom (inspired by "Gita's lore"), and maintaining purity of mind and soul ("Pristine serenity where the soul resides").
